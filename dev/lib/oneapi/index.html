oneAPI extension

Loaded with using oneAPI. It accepts the sparse matrices of oneMKL, routes the level-3 BLAS operations of large fronts (gemm, syrk/herk, trsm and their strided batches) to oneMKL, and lets the regime-A subtree kernels use the 64 KiB local-memory class on Intel GPUs. Every oneMKL call waits for its queue and the LAPACK routines allocate a scratchpad, so the Cholesky and LU panel factorizations stay on the KernelAbstractions kernels; oneMKL's potrf and getrf are reached only through an explicit impl = :vendor of the internal dense interface, which is not a solver parameter. oneMKL's GPU potrf reports the failing column relative to its 32-column panel; the extension reads the column from the factor's diagonal instead, so potrf!, potrf_info! and the solver's info are exact.

oneAPI.oneMKL.oneSparseMatrixCSR — Method
oneSparseMatrixCSR(A::CSR)

The stored CSR matrix of A (the transposed flag is not applied) as a oneSparseMatrixCSR. One-based arrays are shared; zero-based index arrays are copied and rebased. Batches (nbatch(A) > 1) are rejected.
