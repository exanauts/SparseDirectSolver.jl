# Performance

Time of SparseDirectSolver.jl divided by the time of cuDSS per feature and
phase (BenchmarkTools medians), geometric mean over the benchmark matrices
(below 1 is faster).

![SparseDirectSolver.jl vs cuDSS time ratio per feature](../assets/comparison.png)

The comparison was measured at commit 352271c (2026-11-09) against cuDSS
1.0.0 (CUDSS.jl 1.0) on an NVIDIA H200. The cuDSS matching is set explicitly:
off for every feature except `ldlt_matching` (`"algo5"` on both sides), since
cuDSS 1.0 matches and scales `"S"`/`"H"`/`"G"` matrices by default and
SparseDirectSolver.jl only when `matching_alg` is set. The lower panel of the
plot keeps the MadNLP KKT dumps only. SDS/cuDSS ratios (geometric means):

## Fused pipeline

[`FusedSolver`](@ref SparseDirectSolver.FusedSolver) is the fast path of the
interior-point loop: the analysis builds a plan in which one
dependency-counter kernel factorizes the whole elimination tree per
refactorization (instead of one launch per level and size bin), followed by
the inverses of the diagonal blocks of the fronts, and the solve is a
partitioned-inverse sweep, one dependency-counter kernel per direction. The
feature `cholesky_fused` of the comparison measures it; its analysis phase
includes a stock factorization and building the plans.

It is restricted to what the kernels do: a GPU backend (the
dependency-counter kernels spin on resident workgroups, so the
KernelAbstractions CPU backend is refused), real `"SPD"` matrices and real
`"S"` matrices factored without pivoting, one right-hand side, no uniform
batch, Schur complement, matching, iterative refinement or mixed precision;
each raises `NotSupportedError` (the docstring lists them). Its factorization
is bitwise reproducible and `deterministic_mode = 1` selects the reproducible
forward sweep of its solve (T35).
[`DirectSolver`](@ref) remains the general solver; its `solve_alg = "algo1"`
(below) runs the same solve after the stock factorization, and T37–T38 move
the rest of the pipeline under its `execute!`.

## Partitioned-inverse solve (`solve_alg = "algo1"`)

With `setparam!(solver, "solve_alg", "algo1")` before the `"analysis"`, a
[`DirectSolver`](@ref) solves with the fused solve of the pipeline above after
its own factorization: the analysis sizes the inverse storage of the diagonal
blocks `L₁₁` of every front of width ≤ 256 and one dependency-counter solve
segment over the tree (reported in slot 14 of `"memory_estimates"` and added
to slots 1, 2 and 5); every `"factorization"`/`"refactorization"` ends with
one launch that inverts the blocks of the new factor (no host
synchronization, no allocation); `"solve"` turns each front's triangular solve
into a matrix-vector product inside one kernel per direction, with the fronts
wider than 256 on the vendor dense sweeps. `deterministic_mode = 1` (read at
every solve) selects the pull forward sweep, bitwise reproducible. The
sub-phases `"solve_fwd_perm"` … `"solve_bwd_perm"` run the matching parts of
the fused solve (bitwise equal to `"solve"` under `deterministic_mode = 1`),
iterative refinement (`ir_n_steps`, plain or FGMRES) runs its corrections
through it, and matching (`matching_alg`) wraps it as it wraps the level
sweeps.

Its scope is that of the kernels: real `"SPD"` (and real `"HPD"`), real `"S"`
and `"H"` with `pivot_type = 'N'` (the fused sweeps apply no local pivot order
and no 2×2 pivots), one right-hand side, `solve_mode = 0`, a single matrix
(no uniform batch), no Schur complement mode, and a backend whose resident
workgroups make progress while others spin (CUDA, AMDGPU, oneAPI; not the
KernelAbstractions CPU backend or Metal). Anything else raises
`NotSupportedError` at the analysis, the factorization (`pivot_type` changed
to pivoting) or the solve, never a silent fallback to the level sweeps;
`"algo1"` set after the analysis, or a factorization run with `"default"`
before an `"algo1"` solve, raises too.

In the comparison (`cholesky_algo1`, `ldlt_algo1`) the solve drops from
10.3 ms to 2.33 ms on the 78k-bus condensed KKT system (cuDSS:
1.63 ms), and the geometric mean of the SDS/cuDSS solve ratio from
3.84× (level sweeps, `cholesky_f64`) to 1.83× over the same 15
matrices. `"default"` stays the level sweeps: the `"algo1"` residuals are up to
38× higher without refinement (bcsstk38), the default `"S"` pivoting is
outside its scope, it costs device memory and one launch per factorization,
and it was measured on sm_90 only (the T36 Report in `TASKS.md` has the
measurements behind that choice).
